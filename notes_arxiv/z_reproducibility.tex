\section{Reproducibility statement}

We have made several efforts to ensure the reproducibility of our results. All datasets used for training and evaluation are publicly available, as described in Section~\ref{sec:experiments} and Appendix \ref{appx:benchmark}. We provide full training and evaluation code, including our implementation of \method and the ablation variants, in the supplementary materials. Implementation details, including hyperparameters, model checkpoints, and compute requirements, are documented in Appendix \ref{appx:implementation overview}, \ref{appx: ablation model implementations} and \ref{appx:dataset}. Our discriminator's training data, architecture, and evaluation protocol are described in Appendix \ref{appx:discriminator}.
