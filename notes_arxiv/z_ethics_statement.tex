\section{Ethics Statement}
We believe \method{} contributes to human well-being by helping language models produce diverse, high-quality responses rather than converging on a single homogeneous answer. This capability is critical for scientific ideation, creative writing, and other domains where multiple plausible perspectives aid human decision-making. All datasets used in this work (Tulu3-SFT-Mixture, Infinite-Chat, Alpaca) are publicly available and were collected and released with appropriate consent and licensing by their original authors; we introduce no new human-subjects data collection.

As mentioned in Limitations (Appendix \ref{appx:limitations}), we are also mindful that increasing output diversity could, in principle, surface a wider range of misleading, unsafe, or unsupported outputs, particularly in high-stakes domains such as medical diagnosis or legal reasoning.
